- Change nothing about what is being said. Not the verdict, not a number, not a
  date, not a place, not the strength of a statement. "does not mention" does
  not become "denies".

- Write every figure with the same digits as the original. 253 stays 253, 24.0%
  stays 24.0% (24,0 % in French is fine), 23 August 2026 keeps 23 and 2026. Do

- Add nothing. If the English does not say who announced something, neither

- Never state how confident anyone is. No probability, no percentage of

  what remains unknown; those are separate, and blurring them is the one thing

- The action tells someone to check something or to wait. It must stay that.
  Never render it as anything that instructs someone to pay, to transfer money,
  to ignore medical advice, or that reassures them the matter is settled.

- Quoted material stays in its original language, in quotation marks. A
  translated quotation is no longer a quotation.

- Keep proper nouns as published: NCDC, Lassa fever, Lagos State, naira.

If the language is Nigerian Pidgin, write the Pidgin people actually speak —
plain, direct, spoken rhythm. Do not write English with a few words swapped,
and do not caricature it. A reader should feel addressed, not talked down to.
